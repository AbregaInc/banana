# Banana IA decision: Workouts + Nutrition top-level, Rhythm retired

Recorded here so a "listening + movement" review does not creep back as a sixth tab.
This is the decision record for the September 2026 information-architecture change:
Rhythm is retired as a screen on both platforms, iOS gets dedicated Workouts and
Nutrition tabs, and Routines moves under Plan's "…" menu on iOS (it keeps its own
seat on the Mac sidebar).

## Diagnosis

"Rhythm isn't working for me" = the two things used daily (log a meal / tick a
supplement; look at the workout just done) were hidden as the last two cards of a
weekly-aggregate scroll (`Banana/Views/RhythmView.swift:117-118`, `:256-279`), below a
hero, a 4-tile grid, a sleep chart and a "noticed" card. The screen defaulted to "This
week" (`Banana/Model/RhythmPeriod.swift:36`) so on a Monday it read "0 workouts / — /
Rhythm is learning your week" (`RhythmView.swift:284`); its workout count came from
`health.workouts`, a cache only `FitnessView` ever refreshed
(`Banana/Views/FitnessView.swift:40-42` was the sole `refreshIfStale` caller), while
its sleep chart was live (`Banana/Model/HealthKitStore.swift:737-762`) and the
briefing read stale `trends` — so screens disagreed, which read as "broken". It cost
6 HealthKit queries per appear, welded podcast counts to HRV for historical reasons
(commit 0ef9a13), and compared a partial current week against a full previous one
(`RhythmView.swift:294-329`), so its only prose was noise. Nothing in docs, tests or
ASO named a decision Rhythm helped make; the ASO treatment already tested "workouts"
in its slot (the ASO experiment plan, since moved out of the tree). Fix: stop routing daily tools
through a weekly review — make them tabs, delete the review, re-home the three pieces
of it that are worth keeping.

## iOS tab bar (5 tabs, no More overflow)

| # | Tab | Symbol | Root | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan | `sun.max.fill` | `PlannerTab()` | Unchanged. "…" menu (`Banana/Views/WeekView.swift`) gains **Routines** (sheet) and **Morning briefing** (sheet) above Settings. |
| 2 | Workouts | `figure.run` | `NavigationStack { FitnessView() }` | Retitled "Workouts". Adds a period summary strip (workouts · active h · distance, salvaged from Rhythm), a "Guided plans" row (the former Wellness "Coach" segment), and "Recap" in the workout detail toolbar. |
| 3 | Nutrition | `fork.knife` | `NavigationStack { WellnessView() }` | Retitled "Nutrition"; segments reduced to **Food \| Supplements** (Coach leaves). Scan / Photo / Manual are 2 taps from launch. |
| 4 | Notes | `quote.bubble.fill` | `NotesView()` | Unchanged. |
| 5 | Library | `square.stack.fill` | `PodcastsView()` | Unchanged; bottom accessory untouched. |

Routines is demoted on iOS only: it is configuration edited weekly at most, and its
Smart-schedule toggle is a diagnostic, not a daily control
(`Banana/Views/RoutinesView.swift:62-66`). Plan's "…" menu already presents sheets.

## Mac sidebar

`Plan ⌘1 · Workouts ⌘2 · Notes ⌘3 · Routines ⌘4 · Library ⌘5 · Settings` (Routines
keeps its seat — no tab cap on Mac). **Nutrition** is inserted after Workouts only
when wellness sync lands (a later, deferred package); until then the case exists
behind `#if DEBUG` and is filtered out of the sidebar, so the Mac never shows
last-restore meals the way Rhythm showed last-visit counts. Shortcuts are derived
from sidebar position, so the insertion renumbers nothing by hand. Menu bar gains
**Morning Briefing ⌘⇧M**.

## Daily flows after the change

| Flow | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Log meal (barcode) | Rhythm → scroll → card → Scan = 3 taps + scroll | Nutrition → Scan = 2 taps |
| Log meal (manual, saved) | 5 taps + scroll | 4 taps |
| Tick a supplement | 4 taps + scroll | 3 taps |
| Today's workout | 3 taps + scroll | Workouts tab → row = 2 taps |
| Post-workout analysis | n/a | 0 taps on reopen; Workouts → row → Recap later |
| Start a guided plan | 5 taps + scroll | Workouts → Guided plans → plan → Start = 4 |
| Edit a routine | 1 tap | Plan → … → Routines = 3 taps (only regression, accepted) |
| Briefing on demand | unreachable outside the 10-min wake window | Plan → … → Morning briefing; Mac ⌘⇧M |
| Mac workouts | ⌘2 Rhythm | ⌘2 Workouts |

## What shipped alongside this IA change

Two pieces of Rhythm's content were re-homed into standalone features rather than
dropped, and both are already implemented in the codebase:

- **Post-workout review** — `Banana/Views/PostWorkoutReviewView.swift` carries
  Rhythm's Recovery (RHR/HRV vs 7-day mean) and sleep-chart content into a per-workout
  recap, reachable from the workout detail
  toolbar, instead of a weekly rollup.
- **Mac morning briefing** — `MorningBriefingSchedule.swift` brings the briefing (with
  its sleep chart) to the Mac on a schedule (first activation of the day, 05:00–11:00),
  reachable on demand via ⌘⇧M, mirroring the iOS wake-triggered briefing.
