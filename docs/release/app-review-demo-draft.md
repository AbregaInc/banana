# App Review demo instructions — owner review required

This is draft copy, not a message sent to Apple or a submitted review. The selected App Store builds remain unchanged until a verified demo build is prepared and reviewed by the owner. Both platforms retain manual release.

## Proposed reviewer walkthrough

On the welcome screen, choose **Try with sample data**. An existing user can also find it in **Settings → About**. No sign-in is needed for this local sample workspace. A Demo banner identifies it throughout.

- **Plan:** switch between Day and Week, search, change calendar visibility, create/edit/delete sample events, and complete sample reminders or routines. The Alex calendar is intentionally availability-only and read-only.
- **Body:** explore sample workouts, heart-rate and sleep charts. Start a clearly labeled simulated workout, pause it or use its rest timer, then save it to the sample history. In Food, log a manual meal, change its eaten time or reuse a past meal.
- **Notes:** write/edit/archive sample notes and search them. A new note can retain a local draft.
- **Library:** play system-voice narration of app-authored sample text, change its speed, mark it listened or create an episode-anchored note.

Edits survive closing/reopening the demo. **Demo options → Reset sample data** restores the fictional records. **Exit demo** returns to the personal workspace without completing onboarding or connecting an account.

## Access limitation to disclose

This sample workspace uses local providers and does not send Google writes/invitations, exercise server sync/sharing, record through Apple Health or Watch, analyze meal photos with AI, or stream real podcasts. Those features use the normal app and their appropriate account/device permissions. It is not a substitute for proving that those integrations work.

Before relying on this instead of reviewer credentials, ask App Review whether this disclosed sample workspace is sufficient for reviewing this app, or which additional account/device access they require. Google sign-in remains the normal account path; no fake sign-in or hidden review-only bypass is introduced.

## Proposed question to Apple (do not send without owner authorization)

> Banana uses Google OAuth for connected calendar features. We have added an openly accessible, labeled sample-data workspace with editable fictional calendars, reminders, routines, food, notes, workout/sleep charts and sample narration. It requires no account and cannot change real records. Live Google, Health/Watch, photo analysis and podcast integrations remain outside that workspace. May App Review use this workspace for review, and what additional access or evidence would you require for the live integrations?
