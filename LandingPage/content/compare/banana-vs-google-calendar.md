| Feature | Banana Calendar | Google Calendar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Personal Google Calendar is free. Workspace plans add features. |
| Where events live | Connect existing Google accounts. Your Google calendars remain the source for their events. | Create, edit and share events directly in your Google account. |
| Booking | Set your weekly hours, notice and buffers, then share a personal link. | Personal Google accounts can create one booking page. Additional appointment features depend on the plan. |
| Tasks | Apple Reminders appear beside appointments in the native apps. | Schedule Google Tasks in Calendar. |
| Browser workflow | View a linked account’s day or week and adjust calendar inclusion and alerts. Use a native app for event edits. | Create appointments, change guests and edit events in the browser. |
