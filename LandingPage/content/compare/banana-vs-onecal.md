| Feature | Banana Calendar | OneCal Starter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | $60 per person per year on annual billing ($5/month). 14-day free trial. |
| Booking links | Share a personal booking link or send a few free times in a message. Set meeting length, weekly hours, minimum notice and buffers between bookings. | Share unlimited one-on-one or collective links. A collective link offers times when all hosts are free. Set meeting length, hours, notice and buffers. |
| Planning your day | See Apple Reminders beside meetings, check weather and travel time, and set wake or wind-down routines around your calendar. | See connected calendars together, check team availability and RSVP to invitations. |
| Calendars you can connect | Calendars from multiple Google accounts | Two calendars per person. Supports Google, Outlook, iCloud and CalDAV. |
| What coworkers see | A personal appointment can appear as **Busy** on your work calendar, with travel time included. Its title, guests and meeting link stay on the original. | Show busy time only or choose which event details to copy. Filter events by title, color, RSVP or working hours. |
| Creating and editing events | Create, move and edit Google events on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Set repeats and change the guest list. | Create, move and edit events in the unified calendar, including recurring meetings. |
| In a browser | Sign in with a Google account you’ve linked to Banana. View your day or week and change which calendars appear or send alerts. [Open Banana on the web](https://app.bananatheapp.com/) | View and edit events, RSVP to invitations and see teammates’ availability. [OneCal’s web calendar](https://www.onecal.io/calendar-view) |
| Native apps | iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022) · [Download the Mac DMG](/download/mac) | iOS, Android and macOS. |

Banana publishes the code for its native apps (GPL-3.0-only) and its service (AGPL-3.0-only). You can inspect how Banana handles a calendar connection. [Banana’s source and licenses](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana/blob/main/LICENSING.md)

For teams that need to run the software on their own servers, OneCal offers an on-premises option with pricing by request.
