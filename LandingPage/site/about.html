About Banana

Many calendars.
One you.

Banana brings Google calendars, reminders, routines, travel time, and weather into one daily plan on iPhone, iPad, and Apple silicon Mac, with a companion Apple Watch app.

Made by Abrega, Inc.

The Banana team publishes engineering notes and product documentation on this site. We explain what the app does, the permissions it needs, and the limits that matter when you use it.

Banana for Mac is available as a direct download. iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch use the public TestFlight beta. Calendar features require a Google account. Optional modules include food, workouts and sleep, notes, and podcasts. Capabilities and permissions vary by device. Banana is for general wellness and planning and does not give medical advice.
