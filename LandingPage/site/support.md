# Support

Write to [support@abrega.com](mailto:support@abrega.com).

## Connecting Google

Banana works with Google Calendar. Connect it at the first start, or in Settings › Accounts & calendars. Add as many Google accounts as you use.

## Your Banana account

Connecting Google makes your Banana account, so a change to your calendar reaches all your devices. There's no password.

To delete it, open Settings › Accounts & calendars › Delete account. Banana asks you to sign in with Google to confirm, then deletes everything we hold about you. You can also remove Banana's access to Google at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Getting Banana

Get Banana on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022) for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The [Mac download](https://bananatheapp.com/download/mac) and the [iPhone and iPad TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/47yxmU8U) are also available. In the Mac download, update controls are in Settings → About → Updates. For feedback, write to us; TestFlight users can also send a screenshot through TestFlight.
