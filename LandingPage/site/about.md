About Banana

# Many calendars. One you.

Banana brings Google calendars, reminders, routines, travel time, and weather into one daily plan on iPhone, iPad, and Apple silicon Mac.

## Made by Abrega, Inc.

The Banana team publishes engineering notes and product documentation on this site. We explain what the app does, the permissions it needs, and the limits that matter when you use it.

Banana is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022) for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Calendar features require a Google account. Email and other modules are planned.
