Keep personal appointments private

Put a dentist appointment on your personal calendar. Banana can reserve the same time on your work calendar with a Busy block. A colleague who only sees your work calendar sees the occupied hour. The dentist’s name stays on the original event.

See how Busy blocks work

Plan beyond the meeting

See reminders beside appointments and check travel time before heading out. On iPhone, take the day’s plan with you. On iPad and Mac, spread the week across the larger screen.

Connect your Google accounts

Choose the calendars that should block time in Settings. Events you create or edit in Banana are saved to Google Calendar. Calendars that live only in Outlook or iCloud are unsupported.
