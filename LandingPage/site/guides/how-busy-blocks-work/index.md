# Block time across Google calendars

Calendar guide · November 24, 2026

Add a personal appointment once. Banana can reserve the same hour on your work calendar, including time to get there. Someone who can only see your work calendar sees **Busy**.

[![A dentist appointment and travel time on Personal reserve one Busy block on Work.](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/guides/busy-example.webp)](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/guides/busy-example.webp)

The work calendar shows the time reserved for the appointment and travel.

## Set up your calendars

1. Connect your personal and work Google accounts to Banana.
2. In Settings, choose which calendars should block time. Busy blocks are enabled when you connect your accounts. You can switch them off here.
3. Add a test appointment to your personal calendar. Open the work calendar on its own and look for **Busy** at the same time.

A shared calendar owned by someone else can receive blocks if you enable it and have permission to write to it. This connection supports Google Calendar.

## What coworkers can see

The work calendar receives a time range. The appointment title, guest list, location and meeting link stay on the original event. **Anyone who already has access to the original keeps that access**, including invited guests.

[![An open Banana event on iPad shows its title, meeting link, location and RSVP controls.](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/guides/event-details.webp)](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/guides/event-details.webp)

The original event in Banana. Its meeting link and location stay off the Busy block.

## Check the week from your side

Your view brings appointments, reminders and routines together. Open the destination calendar on its own to check the block. To confirm what someone else can see, check their Google sharing permissions as well.

[![Banana’s iPad week view shows Dentist alongside meetings, reminders and routines.](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/ipad-plan.webp)](https://bananatheapp.com/assets/img/ipad-plan.webp)

Your own week view includes event titles from calendars you can access.
