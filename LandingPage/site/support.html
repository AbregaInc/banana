Connecting Google

Banana works with Google Calendar. Connect it at the first start, or in Settings › Accounts & calendars. Add as many Google accounts as you use.

Your Banana account

Connecting Google makes your Banana account, so a change to your calendar reaches all your devices. There's no password.

To delete it, open Settings › Accounts & calendars › Delete account. Banana asks you to sign in with Google to confirm, then deletes everything we hold about you. You can also remove Banana's access to Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The beta

Download Banana for Mac, or join the iPhone and iPad TestFlight beta. On Mac, update controls are in Settings → About → Updates. For feedback, write to us; TestFlight users can also send a screenshot through TestFlight.
