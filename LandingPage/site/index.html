Good to know

Why does Banana ask for my Google account?

To show your Google Calendar events in your plan, to save the events you make or change in Banana, and to add Busy blocks across your own calendars so one plan blocks the others. Banana uses what it reads from Google only for that, and never for advertising.

Does it copy my events between calendars?

No. The other calendars get a Busy block for the same hours, travel time included, without the title or details. It's on once you connect and can be turned off in Settings.

Does Banana replace my calendar?

No. Your events stay in Google Calendar. Banana shows them with your reminders, routines, travel and weather, and what you add in Banana is saved to Google Calendar.

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar, with as many Google accounts as you use. Calendars that live only in iCloud or Outlook aren't shown.

What else can I use in Banana?

Workouts and sleep, food, notes and podcasts are optional modules you can switch on. Features and permissions vary by device. Meal estimates are approximate and use Apple Intelligence where your device supports it. Banana does not give medical advice.

Why does Banana need location?

For local weather and travel times, and for workout routes. Forecasts come from Apple Weather and travel times from Apple Maps.
