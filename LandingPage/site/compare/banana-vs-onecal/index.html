Banana vs OneCal

Both apps let someone book time with you. Choose Banana if you use Google Calendar and want free booking links alongside your reminders, routines and travel time. Choose OneCal if you need one link that checks several hosts’ availability, or you work across Google, Outlook and iCloud.

What does that look like in practice?

Choose Banana if

Your work calendar looks open during a personal appointment. You want it to show Busy while the appointment details stay on your personal Google calendar.

Choose OneCal if

A client gives you an Outlook account, your own meetings live in Google, and people book you through both calendars.
