# Banana *vs* Vimcal

Choose Banana if meetings are one part of a day that also includes errands, routines and travel. Choose Vimcal if much of your day goes into finding meeting times for other people, especially across time zones. Both let you share availability without typing out every slot.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Vimcal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Vimcal lists plans starting at $300/year or $35/month after a trial. |
| Offering times | Share a personal booking link or send free times as a message. | Share available slots or use personal booking links. |
| Time zones | See destination hours alongside your own while traveling. | Use time-zone tools to find a meeting time across cities. |
| Daily work | Edit Google events and see Apple Reminders, weather and travel alongside them. | Meeting priorities, tags and scheduling tools support meeting-heavy calendars. Vimcal also offers a plan for executive assistants. |

## Whose schedule are you coordinating?

### Choose Banana if

You are sending a client your own openings and want the drive to your next meeting accounted for.

### Choose Vimcal if

You coordinate an executive’s calendar and repeatedly negotiate times with people in different cities.
