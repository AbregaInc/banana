# Banana *vs* Notion Calendar

Both calendars are free and can block busy time across calendars. Choose Banana if you plan around Apple Reminders, travel and personal routines. Choose Notion Calendar if you want to move a project deadline on your calendar and update the underlying Notion database item.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Notion Calendar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Notion Calendar is free. |
| Alongside your meetings | Apple Reminders, travel time, weather and routines. | Notion deadlines, project timelines and linked meeting documents. |
| Scheduling | Send available times or a personal booking link with notice and buffers. | Built-in scheduling lets you share availability and a scheduling link. |
| Conflicts | Private Busy blocks reserve time on other enabled Google calendars. | Automatically block busy slots across connected calendars. |
| Editing | Create and edit Google events on iPhone, iPad and Mac. | Manage calendar events and drag Notion database items to change their dates. |

Checked November 24, 2026. [Notion Calendar features and availability](https://www.notion.com/product/calendar) · [Banana Calendar features](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022).

## Where does a change of plan need to show up?

### Choose Banana if

A meeting moves across town. You want the updated event, the journey and your next reminder in the same day view.

### Choose Notion Calendar if

A launch slips a week. You want to drag its database item to a new date and see that change in your workspace.
