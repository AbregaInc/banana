# Banana *vs* Fantastical

Both apps bring appointments and tasks into one view. Choose Banana if you use Google calendars and want personal booking links, Apple Reminders and travel time for free. Choose Fantastical if your calendars span providers or you want to propose several meeting times and let invitees vote.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Fantastical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Free calendar tier. Fantastical Scheduling comes with Flexibits Premium. |
| Calendar accounts | Multiple Google accounts. Create and edit events in the native apps. | Connect accounts from services including Google, iCloud and Microsoft Exchange. |
| Scheduling | Share a personal booking link or send available times as a message. | Premium includes Openings for bookings and Proposals for finding a time with invitees. |
| Planning horizon | Day and week views with meetings, Apple Reminders, weather and travel time. | Day, week, month, quarter and year views let you zoom out for longer-range planning. |

## How do you get a meeting on the calendar?

### Choose Banana if

A client needs half an hour with you. You send your link, then check your reminders and the drive to your next appointment in the same plan.

### Choose Fantastical if

Three people need to agree on a time. You want to propose a few options and collect their responses.
