# Banana *vs* Calendly

For a client booking time with you, both apps offer a free link. Choose Banana if you also want to plan the rest of your day across Google accounts. Choose Calendly if incoming bookings need to be qualified, routed or distributed across a team.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Calendly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Free individual plan. Team scheduling and routing are paid features. |
| Personal booking | Share a booking link with your hours, meeting length, minimum notice and buffers. | Free includes a customizable booking page, one event type and one calendar connection. |
| Availability | Connect multiple Google accounts and reserve commitments across enabled calendars with private Busy blocks. | Paid Standard supports multiple calendar connections. Teams adds lead qualification and routing. |
| After the booking | Create and edit Google events, check travel time and see Apple Reminders on your day plan. | Paid plans offer booking workflows such as automated reminders and follow-ups. |

Checked November 24, 2026. [Calendly plans and booking features](https://calendly.com/pricing) · [Banana Calendar features](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022).

## Who should receive the booking?

### Choose Banana if

A client is booking you specifically. Your link should respect your availability, and you want the meeting in the same planner as your other commitments.

### Choose Calendly if

A prospect needs any available member of the right sales team. You want the booking flow to decide who gets the meeting.
