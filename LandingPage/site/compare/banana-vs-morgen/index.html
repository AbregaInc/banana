Banana vs Morgen

Choose Banana if your to-dos live in Apple Reminders and you want them beside your Google meetings. Choose Morgen if you pull work from several task lists and want to assign it time on your calendar. Both offer booking links.

Feature Banana Calendar Morgen
Price Free Morgen Pro is $15/month billed yearly or $30 billed monthly, with a 14-day trial.
Tasks and time See Apple Reminders alongside appointments and routines. Bring calendars and task lists together and time-block work.
Booking Personal booking links and available times you can send in a message. Booking links based on calendar availability are included in Pro.
Devices Native apps on iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac, plus browser access for linked accounts. Pro includes desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux.
