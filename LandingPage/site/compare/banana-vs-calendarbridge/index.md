# Banana *vs* CalendarBridge

Banana brings Google calendars and Apple Reminders into one daily plan. CalendarBridge connects work calendars across providers and lets you choose which event details travel with them.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| What you need | Banana Calendar | CalendarBridge Basic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protect an appointment | Reserve time on enabled Google calendars without copying the title, guests or meeting link | Sync events across calendars and choose which details are shared, including busy-only |
| Browser access | [Banana web app](https://app.bananatheapp.com/): sign in with a linked Google account to view day/week plans and change calendar inclusion and alerts. Use a native app to create or edit events | CalendarBridge offers its unified calendar on the web, iPhone and Android |
| Share availability | Send available times or share a booking link | Create up to ten scheduling pages |
| Calendar price | Free | Two calendars for $4/month billed yearly, or $5/month billed monthly |
| Get the app | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022) or [direct Mac download (.dmg)](https://bananatheapp.com/download/mac) | [CalendarBridge](https://calendarbridge.com/pricing-individual/) |

## How much should your other calendar see?

### Choose Banana if

You want colleagues to see when you’re busy while the event title, guests and meeting link stay on your original Google calendar.

### Choose CalendarBridge if

You work across Google and Outlook, or need to choose which event details to share between calendars.
