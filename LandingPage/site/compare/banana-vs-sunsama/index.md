# Banana *vs* Sunsama

Choose Banana if you want to open your calendar and see what today already requires. Choose Sunsama if you want a daily planning session where you select tasks, estimate the work and decide what can fit before shutdown.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Sunsama |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Pro is $17/month billed yearly or $22 billed monthly after a 14-day trial. |
| Building the day | Google events, Apple Reminders and routines appear together in the native planner. | Guided daily planning helps you select work and timebox tasks around your calendar. |
| Closing the day | Set a wind-down routine around your calendar and use alarms on supported Apple devices. | Plan around a shutdown time and review the work you carried over. |
| Working around meetings | Share a booking link or free times. Travel time can reserve additional space around an appointment. | Use your calendar to decide where task work fits around meetings. |

## How do you want to start Monday?

### Choose Banana if

You check the morning’s appointments, a reminder due at noon and when to leave for a client visit.

### Choose Sunsama if

You bring tasks in from your work tools, decide how much time to spend on each and deliberately move lower priorities to another day.
