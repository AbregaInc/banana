# Banana *vs* Reclaim

Choose Banana if you want to plan around fixed commitments and keep personal time blocked on your work calendar. Choose Reclaim if you want software to find and defend time for flexible habits, tasks and focus sessions when meetings move.

[Get Banana](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/banana-daily-planner/id6792318022)[Open source](https://github.com/AbregaInc/banana)

## Side by side

| Feature | Banana Calendar | Reclaim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Reclaim offers a free Lite plan and paid plans with higher limits. |
| Protecting time | A Busy block can reserve a personal commitment and its travel time across enabled Google calendars. | Calendar Sync shares availability. Focus Time protects blocks for work. |
| Routines | Set wake and wind-down routines around your calendar. | Habits finds time for recurring activities around other commitments. |
| Booking | Share a personal booking link or send specific available times. | Scheduling Links offer times using Reclaim’s scheduling controls. |

## What should happen when a meeting moves?

### Choose Banana if

You need to see the new meeting time, how long it takes to get there and which reminders are still due.

### Choose Reclaim if

You want the scheduler to find another opening for the focus session that just lost its slot.
