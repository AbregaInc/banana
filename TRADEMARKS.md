# Banana name and artwork

The GPLv3 and AGPLv3 grants cover code under their stated scope. They do not grant
trademark rights in the Banana name, logos or mascot, or a licence to redistribute
the original app icons and promotional artwork. Third-party artwork retains its
owners' rights and any separately stated licence.

You may refer to Banana by name to describe the project or identify the origin
of a fork. Do not imply that a modified build is an official or endorsed release.
Use your own name and appropriately licensed artwork for redistributed builds
unless you have separate permission from the relevant rights holders.

A public source release must provide usable replacement fixture artwork and
document which brand assets, if any, are included with separate permission.

The synthetic sample-audio covers in `Banana/PreviewArtwork/` are explicitly
GPL-3.0-only fixture artwork; they are not reserved promotional/app-icon assets.
