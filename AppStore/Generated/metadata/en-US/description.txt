Many calendars. One you.

Banana brings your Google calendars into one daily plan, with reminders, routines, weather and travel time alongside them.

See your calendar, reminders, routines, weather and travel time together—then log what you eat, what you listen to and how you move in the same place. Start the day knowing what is happening, what needs attention and where the open space is.

PLAN THE DAY
• Connect Google Calendar and see every account on one timeline, by day or by week
• Add an event the way you would say it—"Lunch with Sam Friday at 1"—then edit, move or repeat it, and add or remove guests
• Keep Apple Reminders beside your meetings: tick one off with an Undo, open it to read it in full, and find anything overdue at the top of today
• Account for weather, travel time and when to leave; on a trip, see the hours where you are going beside your own
• Set smart wake and wind-down routines, and start a routine yourself, in the app or with Siri

Food, Notes, Podcasts and Workouts and sleep are optional modules. Turn them on in Settings > Modules.

EAT WITHOUT THE BOOKKEEPING
• Log a meal from a photo, a barcode, a sentence or a search of the USDA food database
• Pick a past meal when you forgot the photo, and edit when you ate
• Scan a package once and it becomes one of your foods, at your usual amount
• Tick off supplements and medication, on a schedule or after a moment such as your workout
• See the day's energy and macros, and look back through past days

LISTEN WITH CONTEXT
• Search, subscribe, download and play podcasts, each show at its own speed
• Follow word-by-word transcripts made on your device, and have them say who is speaking
• Save voice notes connected to the moment that inspired them
• See listening history and the places connected to it

MOVE, SLEEP AND RECOVER
• Start workouts from Apple Watch, pause and resume them, and keep the metrics readable while moving
• Review routes, splits, heart-rate zones and trends
• Trim a workout that ran long, on any device, and Apple Health follows
• See each night's sleep and its wake-ups
• Bring Apple Health data into the same picture of your week

WORK ACROSS YOUR DEVICES
Banana runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, and keeps your library, progress, routines, meals and settings in step through your Banana account. Health sync is optional and requires separate consent. Start a workout on your wrist, listen from your pocket and plan the week at your desk.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Transcription runs on your device. Meal estimates use Apple Intelligence on your device or Apple’s Private Cloud Compute when available. Banana’s service supports account sync and calendar notifications. Banana asks for access only when a feature needs it, and explains why before the system prompt appears.

The Mac app requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. Calendar features need a Google account. Estimating a meal from a photo or a sentence uses Apple Intelligence where the device supports it. Banana describes your health data; it does not give medical advice.
