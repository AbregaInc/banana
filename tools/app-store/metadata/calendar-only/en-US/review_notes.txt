This build is the calendar alone: Plan (Google Calendar, Apple Reminders, routines, alarms, event alerts, weather, travel time) and Settings. It has no Workouts, Sleep, Food, Notes, Podcasts or Email, no Apple Watch app, and no HealthKit, background audio, background location, camera, microphone, photos or speech access. There are no purchases.

QUICK REVIEW: SAMPLE WORKSPACE (NO SIGN-IN)
1. On the welcome screen choose "Explore an example". A Demo banner marks this fictional, local workspace.
2. Switch Day and Week, change calendar visibility, and create, edit or delete sample events. Complete a reminder or routine. The Alex calendar shows availability only and is read-only.
3. Edits survive reopening. Demo options > Reset sample data restores the records. Exit demo returns to the personal workspace.

LIVE GOOGLE CALENDAR REVIEW
Exit the demo. Choose "Continue with Google" during setup, or Settings > Accounts & calendars > Connect Google Calendar, and use the account in App Review Sign-In Information. After allowing Calendar access, test Day and Week, visibility, and creating, editing and deleting an event.

Account deletion is in Settings > Accounts & calendars > Delete account and requires fresh authentication.

Weather shows Apple Weather attribution and its legal link on the forecast. Location is used while the app is in use for the forecast and travel time. "Learn from my trips" (Settings > Travel) is off until turned on; only then does Banana ask for Always location and Motion, to learn real travel times from places visited. It uses visit monitoring, not continuous background location.
