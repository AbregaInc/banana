Build 117 is a new, smaller app: the calendar alone. It has Plan (Google Calendar, Apple Reminders, routines, alarms, event alerts, weather, travel time), Booking and Settings. Workouts, Food, Notes, Podcasts, Email and the Apple Watch app are not in this build. There are no purchases.

HOW EARLIER ISSUES ARE RESOLVED IN THIS BUILD
- 2.5.4 audio: no podcast player and no "audio" background mode.
- 2.5.4 location: no background or Always location, no visit monitoring and no Motion. Location is When In Use only, for the forecast and travel time. Background modes are only remote notifications and background fetch, which refresh the calendar.
- 2.5.1 HealthKit: no HealthKit entitlement, no Health usage strings and no Health features.
- 5.2.5 WeatherKit: in Plan, tap the temperature beside the date (Mac: click it in the toolbar). The forecast ends with the Apple Weather mark and the "Other data sources" link to Apple's legal attribution page. Settings > About > Credits also links it.
- 2.4.5 (Mac): the com.apple.security.network.server entitlement and the Bonjour/local network keys are removed. The Mac app makes outgoing connections only.

QUICK REVIEW, NO SIGN-IN
1. On the welcome screen choose "Explore an example" (or Settings > About > Try with sample data). A Demo banner marks this fictional, local workspace.
2. Switch Day and Week, change calendar visibility, create, edit and delete sample events, complete a reminder or routine. The Alex calendar shows availability only and is read-only.
3. Demo options > Reset sample data restores it. Exit demo returns to your own setup.

LIVE GOOGLE CALENDAR
Exit the demo. Choose "Continue with Google" on the calendar step, or Settings > Accounts & calendars > Connect Google Calendar, and use the Google account in App Review Sign-In Information. Allow Calendar access, then test Day and Week, visibility, and creating, editing and deleting an event. "Not now" on the same step skips Google; routines, alarms and reminders work without it.

GUIDELINE 4.8
Banana is a client for Google Calendar. Its only sign-in is Google's own, and every feature that needs it works on the person's own Google Calendar through the Google Calendar API. Banana has no account, username or password of its own and offers no other sign-in. We ask that the 4.8 exception for clients of a specific third-party service apply.
Booking (Settings > Booking) is part of this: the booking page offers only the times free on the person's Google Calendar and writes each booking into it as a Google Calendar event, so it cannot work without the Google sign-in. Behind that sign-in, Banana's server also relays Google's calendar change notifications to the person's devices and keeps routines and settings in step between them. It holds no profile. Settings > Accounts & calendars > Delete account removes it, confirmed with a fresh Google sign-in.

PERMISSIONS, ALL OPTIONAL
Calendar (Google), Reminders, Notifications, Alarms (AlarmKit, for wake alarms), Contacts (picking guests) and Location When In Use. Each can be declined.
