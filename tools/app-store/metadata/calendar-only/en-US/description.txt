Banana puts your Google calendars, Apple Reminders and routines on one timeline, with weather and travel time beside them.

PLAN THE DAY
• Every Google account on one timeline, by day or by week
• Add an event the way you'd say it: "Lunch with Sam Friday at 1"
• Move, repeat and edit events; add or remove guests
• Reminders beside your meetings, overdue ones at the top of today
• Weather for where you'll be, travel time and when to leave
• On a trip, the hours where you're going beside your own

ROUTINES AND ALARMS
• Wake and wind-down routines that follow your calendar
• Reminders due after a moment, such as waking or a meeting
• Alarms that ring through silent mode and Focus
• Alerts before each event

ON EVERY DEVICE
iPhone, iPad and Mac, kept in step through your Banana account. Widgets show what's next.

Calendar features need a Google account. The Mac app requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
