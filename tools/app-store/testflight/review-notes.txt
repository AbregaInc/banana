Banana combines a daily planner with optional Workouts and sleep, Food, Notes and Podcasts modules. Plan is always available. Enable the optional modules in Settings > Modules; they are available to all users. The main app does not list Email and excludes its separate experimental implementation and Gmail scopes. Guided workout coaching is disabled; existing records are retained for compatibility. There is no purchase flow in this build.

A Google connection is optional for local planning, notes, food and podcast use. Google-connected calendar features require an account. For review without a Google account, choose Try with sample data on the welcome screen, or Settings > About > Try with sample data on an existing install. This opens an isolated fictional workspace: create/edit a sample calendar event, review availability-only events, and try the sample tasks, meals, notes, workouts and listening. Reset sample data and Exit sample data are explicit. Simulated workouts and system-voice narration are labeled; they are not successful Google or Health connections. Live integrations still require their normal accounts and permissions. No Google review credentials are supplied; if live integration credentials are required, they must be provided separately. Calendar access is through Google's Calendar APIs. The Banana account uses that Google identity to support cross-device sync and calendar change notifications. Account deletion is available in Settings > Accounts & calendars after fresh authentication, and revokes server access and deletes the account's service data according to the privacy policy.

Banana's service stores the account and sync data described in the privacy policy. Health and food sync require separate consent; without it, they stay on the device. Withdraw Health sync consent in Settings > Accounts & calendars to remove the service copy. The legacy health iCloud transports are read-only for migration; new health changes use consented Banana service sync. Existing local data is preserved.

Apple Watch workout recording requires Health access on the paired Watch. The app displays Health data and workout/sleep summaries; it is not a diagnostic or treatment tool. Medication timing information does not determine a safe schedule or offer automatic rescheduling when medication is logged; the person is directed to their doctor or pharmacist.

Permissions are requested for the relevant feature, and optional permissions can be declined. Transcription runs on the device. Meal estimates use Apple Intelligence, on-device or Apple's Private Cloud Compute where available; the capture screens describe the processing route. Food search and barcode lookup use a bundled catalogue and public food databases. Weather uses Apple Weather attribution. Some user-selected podcast feeds and media use HTTP; Banana's own service and Google connections use HTTPS.

This TestFlight build is the main Banana app. It has the same optional module policy as the intended public app; Email remains unavailable. The separate development-installed Banana Beta app has its own identity and storage and is not this TestFlight binary.
