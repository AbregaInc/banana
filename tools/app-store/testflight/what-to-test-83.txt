Build 83 is the App Store release candidate. It carries everything from build 82 plus podcast safety changes: hardened progress transfer between your devices, episode ownership checks, and bounded transcript parsing. Try with sample data is on the welcome screen and in Settings > About; sample data is isolated from your accounts. The Mac app requires Apple silicon.

Please check the release candidate on your usual devices:

• Calendar: switch Day/Week, create an event by typing a sentence such as "Lunch with Sam Friday at 1", edit it, and check recurring and shared calendars. Another person's availability must remain visible when no detailed source is available.
• Podcasts: subscribe to a show, download an episode, open its transcript, and scroll a large history. Episodes hidden by speaker/voice preferences should be absent.
• Food: pick a past meal without taking a new photo, edit the time you ate, and confirm the saved meal on another device.
• Notes: save and edit a note, then check its Apple Reminder. A pending write-back should not create a duplicate reminder on retry.
• Workouts: dismiss a completed workout summary, background and reopen the app. It should stay dismissed.
• Watch: check the complication on your usual face and in Night Mode.
• Privacy: a new install should leave optional usage/diagnostic sharing off until you choose it.

Known issue: some Busy copies on Google-shared calendars still remain separate; Calendar diagnostics has a shareable report.

Plan is always enabled. Food, Notes, Podcasts and Workouts and sleep can be enabled in Settings > Modules. Email is unavailable in this app.

Report problems with TestFlight feedback or support@abrega.com.
