Many calendars. One you.

Banana brings Google calendars, reminders, routines, weather and travel time into one daily plan on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with workouts and listening on Apple Watch.

Choose optional Food, Notes, Podcasts and Workouts and sleep modules in Settings > Modules. Email is not included in this main Banana TestFlight build. The separate Banana Beta development app has different capabilities and storage.

Calendar features require a Google account. Cross-device account sync uses Banana’s service; Health sync requires separate consent. Usage and diagnostic sharing are optional.
