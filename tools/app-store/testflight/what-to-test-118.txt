Build 118 makes Plan feel more like an iPhone, iPad and Mac app should.

Please try:
• What needs attention now sits with your reminders and routines at the top of the day; tick it there.
• Today appears only after you page away from today. On a small iPhone the weather takes its place.
• iPad and Mac: Plan's toolbar has three groups, and the sidebar hides and shows (⌃⌘S).
• Mac: Day, 3/5 days and Week (⌥⌘1/3/5/7), Previous/Next (⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→), Today (⌘T) and search (⌘F) in the menus; Settings with ⌘,.
• Larger, clearer small text; the calendar follows your text size.
• Sheets that only show something close with the system ×.

Plan is always on; Workouts and sleep, Food, Notes and Podcasts are in Settings > Modules. Report problems with TestFlight feedback or support@abrega.com.
