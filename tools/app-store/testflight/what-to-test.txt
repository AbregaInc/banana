Build 82 adds the approved dimensional Banana artwork, a clearer glyph for tinted Watch faces, and Try with sample data from the welcome screen or Settings > About. Sample data is isolated from your accounts. The Mac app requires Apple silicon.

Please check the Watch complication on your usual face and Night Mode. Physical Watch appearance has not yet been verified for this build.

Please check the release candidate on your usual devices:

• Calendar: switch Day/Week, create and edit an event, and check recurring and shared calendars. Busy copies should merge with their detailed source when the identity is known; another person’s availability must remain visible when no detailed source is available.
• Food: pick a past meal without taking a new photo, edit the time you ate, and confirm the saved meal on another device. Medication timing information should not automatically move your supplements.
• Notes: save and edit a note, then check its Apple Reminder. If write-back is pending, the note should remain saved and retry should not create a duplicate reminder.
• Podcasts: scroll a large history. Episodes hidden by speaker/voice preferences should be absent, and paging should use the eligible episodes.
• Workouts: dismiss a completed workout summary, background and reopen the app. It should stay dismissed.
• Privacy: a new install should leave optional usage/diagnostic sharing off until you choose it.

Known issue: some Busy copies on Google-shared calendars still remain separate. That investigation is deferred; this build adds a shareable report in Calendar diagnostics and does not claim to resolve every shared-calendar case.

Plan is always enabled. Food, Notes, Podcasts and Workouts and sleep can be enabled in Settings > Modules without preview labels. Email is unavailable in this main app.
