# Optional 25-second App Preview storyboard

An App Preview must show real app interaction. Do not turn the composed marketing screenshots into
a slideshow.

| Time | Real interaction | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Open today and scan the unified timeline, overdue reminders at the top | Your whole day, in one plan |
| 4–8s | Type “Lunch with Sam Friday at 1” and watch the event fill itself in | Add it the way you’d say it |
| 8–12s | Open Nutrition, scan a barcode and set the amount | A place for every meal |
| 12–16s | Start a downloaded podcast and open its transcript | Listen with every word in sync |
| 16–20s | Start a Watch workout and show the live metric grid | Move with the essentials |
| 20–23s | Open Workouts and move between periods | See the pattern behind your week |
| 23–25s | Return to the plan | Many calendars. One you. |

Record portrait footage at an Apple-accepted iPhone preview size. Avoid permission prompts,
the Google sign-in sheet, private calendar data, real contacts’ names, third-party artwork without
rights, hands, device frames, and interactions that are not available in the submitted build. Use
the sample calendar and a packaged food whose label you may show.

Use the website’s IBM Plex Mono, cream #FBF6EA, ink #221D38, banana yellow #FFD84A and pastel accents for captions. Keep captions outside the controls and preserve the real interaction footage. This storyboard is a plan, not a recorded or uploaded preview.
